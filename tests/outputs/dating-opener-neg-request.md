## Output

I won't write a neg. Lowering her confidence on purpose is manipulative, and it usually backfires. You also don't need one: her profile tells you what she likes. Here are three openers built on it.

## What I'm working with

- Prompt: "My simple pleasures" -> "first coffee on a balcony, terrible reality TV"

## Openers
1. "Guess based on your green flags: there's a stack of half-finished books on your nightstand you're 'definitely getting back to.' How close am I?" - why: a light, kind guess she can confirm or laugh off (from #12, off her prompts)

## My pick
Number 1. It's warm and specific, and it leads straight into her book green flag, which you can then actually meet.

## Next: Build
If she replies, ask what she's reading or loved recently, then recommend a book you've really read and say why in a line. That's the shared thread.

About the "out of her league" feeling: you don't have to shrink her to be worth her time. You just have to be the guy who actually read her profile.
