## Output

## What I'm working with
- **Photos:** (1) on a bike at a farmers market, (2) holding a very fluffy grey cat, (3) a half-finished game of Catan at a board game cafe, (4) hiking in rain gear, soaked and laughing, (5) cooking with a wok.
- **"I'll fall for you if"** → "you can lose at board games gracefully. I cannot."
- **"My most irrational fear"** → "that my cat secretly prefers my neighbour"
- **"Together, we could"** → "find the best dumplings in the city and rank them with a spreadsheet"
- **Details:** 27, looking for long-term, drinks sometimes. Her tone is playful and self-aware.
- **Best hook:** She admits she's a sore loser at board games, and photo 3 shows a Catan game that was never finished. Her prompt and her photo point to the same thing, and the prompt asks you to reply.

## Openers
1. "'I cannot lose gracefully' is a bold admission. That half-finished Catan in photo 3: evidence or crime scene?" - why: it answers her prompt and connects it to her photo, so she can reply with a funny story (from #7, off her prompts)
2. "Guess based on your board game prompt: you're the one who puts the robber on whoever's winning, no apologies. How close am I?" - why: a specific, friendly guess she can confirm or deny, and it plays along with the joke she made about herself (from #12, off her prompts)
3. "Dumpling spreadsheet, fully on board. What's the top scoring category: wrapper, filling, or dipping sauce?" - why: it agrees with her plan and adds a tiny, low-stakes debate. It's also the easiest one to answer (from #11, off her prompts)

## My pick
**Number 1.** It's the only one that shows you read her prompt *and* looked at her photos. "Crime scene" also gives her an easy opening to tell the story of that game.

## Next: Build
If she replies, stick with board games. Tell her honestly how you handle losing, or name a game you actually play. Save the dumpling spreadsheet for later, because it makes an easy date ask once the chat is going well.
