## Output

## Your lineup

Hinge requires six photos, and only one of yours is strong right now. Here's the best order using what you have, plus two slots to fill this week.

1. **Photo 3 (lead).** Solo, face clear, soft window light, a real laugh. This is exactly what a lead photo should be.
2. **Photo 5.** The summit gives her something to ask about, and it's your only full-body shot. You're tiny and turned away, so it can't carry the profile, but it works as the "what I do" photo.
3. **Photo 6.** Only keep this if fishing is really part of your life. You're grinning and it shows an activity, but the cap and sunglasses hide your face. Keep it mid-pack and never early.
4. **Photo 1.** Your social shot, though it's weak. Use Hinge's crop to frame just you and the guy next to you. If it still looks sharp after the crop, keep it. If it goes grainy, cut it.
5. **New photo: a clear half-body shot** (see Take next)
6. **New photo: another face shot in a different setting** (see Take next). Until you have it, Photo 2 can hold this spot. Swap it out as soon as you can.

## Cut

- **Photo 4:** A shirtless gym-mirror shot with your phone over your face. It hides your face and sends a signal that rarely helps. Cut it today.
- **Photo 7:** Your arm around a woman who's been cropped out, with her hand and hair still in the frame. People read it as an ex. Cut it today.
- **Photo 2:** A car selfie in sunglasses on an overcast day. It's flat, your eyes are hidden, and the car seat is a tired background. Keep it only until you have a replacement.

The pattern behind "no likes": four of your seven photos hide your face (sunglasses, a cap, a phone, a turned back), and the rest of the profile doesn't give anyone a second clear look at you. On Hinge she sees several photos as she scrolls, so one great shot followed by five unclear ones doesn't add up to a picture of who you are.

## Take next

You can get all of these with a friend and a phone in one outing this week:

- **Half-body shot, outdoors.** Go out in the hour before sunset. Have your friend hold the phone at your eye level, then step back until you're framed from the waist up. Wear a well-fitting shirt or overshirt in a solid colour. Have them talk to you and shoot while you're mid-laugh instead of posing.
- **Second face shot, different setting.** Change into a second outfit and find a new backdrop: a street, a park bench or a bar terrace in daylight. Chest-up, no sunglasses or cap, relaxed smile.
- **The summit, redone (next time you're out).** Turn toward the camera and have your friend get closer, so you fill about a third of the frame and the view is still behind you. You keep the story and she gets to see your face.
- **Optional retake of the fish photo.** Same moment with the sunglasses up and the cap off or pushed back.

Prep: get a haircut or beard trim a few days before, and bring two or three outfit changes so the photos don't all look like they're from one afternoon.
