# Test: dating-photo-ranker

## Input (fictional)

(All fictional. Photos described in text because this test runs headless; in real use you upload the images.)

28M, on Hinge, getting basically no likes. Rank my photos? Which one should be first?

Photo 1: Group of five guys at a wedding, I'm second from the left in a navy suit, everyone's small in the frame, indoor flash.
Photo 2: Selfie in my car, overcast light, wearing sunglasses, slight smile.
Photo 3: Close-up of me laughing at a cafe table by a window, soft daylight, face clear, holding a coffee.
Photo 4: Shirtless gym mirror selfie, phone covering part of my face.
Photo 5: Me on a mountain summit, full body, tiny in the frame, back half-turned, big view.
Photo 6: Me holding a big fish on a boat, cap and sunglasses, grinning.
Photo 7: Me with my arm around a woman at a bar, she's cropped out but her hand and hair are visible.


## Output

## Your lineup

Your best photo is buried. Right now only one of your seven photos (Photo 3) clearly shows your face in good light. You're probably not getting likes because of that, not because of you. Hinge asks for six photos, so below is the best order for now, and the shot list fills the gaps.

1. **Photo 3 (lead):** This should be your first photo. You're alone, your face is clear, the window gives soft daylight and you're laughing for real. It's warm and close, which is what you want up front.
2. **Photo 5:** The summit shows an interest and gives her an easy opener ("which mountain?"). It's not your lead because you're tiny and half-turned away, but it works as the activity shot.
3. **Photo 6:** Keep the fish photo only if fishing is genuinely your thing. It shows a hobby and you're grinning. The cap and sunglasses hide your face, though, and fish photos are a common cliché, so swap it out once you have a better hobby shot.
4. **Photo 1, cropped:** Use this as your one social shot. Crop it to you plus one or two of the guys so you're easy to spot. The navy suit helps. Never put it first.
5. **Photo 2:** Keep this as a placeholder only. Replace it as soon as you've taken the new shots.

## Cut

- **Photo 7:** A woman cropped out of the frame, with her hand and hair still showing, reads as "ex" straight away. Remove it today.
- **Photo 4:** Shirtless gym mirror selfie with the phone covering your face. It costs you more than it gains. Cut it entirely.
- **Photo 2 (soon):** Car seat, sunglasses and overcast light. It's the weakest keeper and should be the first one you replace.

## Take next

- **Half-body shot showing your style.** Ask a friend to take it outdoors in the hour before sunset. Phone at chest or eye level, waist-up, no sunglasses. Wear a solid-colour shirt or overshirt that fits well. Talk to them while they shoot and get a laugh mid-sentence instead of posing.
- **Second clear face shot in a new setting,** like a park, a street or a market. Change your outfit so it doesn't look like the same afternoon as the first shot. Get a haircut or beard trim a few days beforehand.
- **Better hobby shot.** Next time you hike or fish, have someone take a photo of you facing the camera from a few metres away, with the sunglasses pushed up or off and the view still behind you. This can replace Photo 6, and maybe Photo 5 as well.
- **Optional personality shot:** cooking, a dog, playing something, travelling. Pick something real that she could ask you about.

Get two or three of these this week and your lineup becomes: 3, new half-body, new hobby, cropped 1, new face shot, 5.
