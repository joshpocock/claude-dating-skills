# The 3-step texting framework: Hook, Build, Ask

Every chat from match to date goes through three steps. The skills use them to decide what the next message should do, and to tell the user where a chat is. The user still writes and sends every message in his own words.

## Step 1: Hook (the opener)

**Job:** open specific. One real detail from her profile plus one easy thing to reply to.

- Under about 25 words, one question max, about her profile, not a speech about him.
- No "hey", no looks compliments, no interview questions ("what do you do?").
- **Done when:** she replies with more than a polite one-liner, or asks something back.
- **Stuck:** no reply. One light, new message after a day or two is fine; after that, let it go.

## Step 2: Build (rapport)

**Job:** turn the opener into a real conversation around one shared thread.

- **One shared thread:** something she cares about that connects to something he really does (from her profile, the chat or his voice profile; never invent an interest).
- **One easy question per message,** after giving something back: answer her properly, add a detail or a take, then hand back one easy thing.
- Match her length and energy. Reference what she said earlier.
- **Ask signals** (move to Step 3 when two or more show up): she asks questions back, her replies are as long as his or longer, she starts topics, she mentions free time, a place or an activity, or she says something like "we should".
- **Do not let it drift.** Usually a handful to a couple of dozen messages. Long pen-pal chats tend to fizzle.
- **Stuck:** one-word answers, no questions back, he is carrying it. Match her energy with something short and low-pressure; do not jump to an ask, and do not chase.

## Step 3: Ask (the date)

**Job:** turn the shared thread into a plan.

- **One specific day + one activity + one kind of place,** tied to the shared thread ("You clearly need proof. Ramen at [a spot near you], Thursday evening?").
- Easy to say yes to or to suggest another time. Public place, first-date length. One ask, no pressure.
- **Yes:** confirm day, time and place, then save the rest for the date.
- **"Maybe" with no alternative:** one friendly line, leave the door open, do not ask again.
- **No, space, or unmatched:** that is the answer. Accept it and stop.

## Which step is this chat at?

| Step | It's here when |
|---|---|
| Hook | No reply yet, or only a short first reply |
| Build | They are talking, but the ask signals are not there yet |
| Ask | Two or more ask signals, or she has already hinted at meeting |

If it is unclear, say so and treat it as Build.

## Never

No games, no negging, no jealousy tactics, no pressure, no guilt, no waiting on purpose before replying, no repeated follow-ups. Nothing gets sent automatically: the user picks, edits and sends his own message.
