# 30 opener templates

Patterns, not scripts. Each template has [slots] that must be filled from something real on HER profile (and, where a slot asks for something about him, from his voice profile or what he told you). If a slot cannot be filled with a real detail, do not use that template. Never send one generic or word for word: rewrite it in his voice, at his length, with his punctuation and humour.

What makes them work: a specific detail she chose to put on her profile, a light take, and one easy thing to reply to. Every template follows the Hook rules in `texting-framework.md`.

## Off her photos (describe the activity, place, pet or object, never her looks)

1. "Important question about photo [n]: is the [animal or object in the photo] yours, or a very convincing prop?"
2. "[Activity in the photo] in [conditions you can see, e.g. that much rain]. Dedicated or slightly unhinged?"
3. "That [dish in the photo] looks serious. Did you make it, or are you just very good at ordering?"
4. "You look way too relaxed for someone [doing the activity, e.g. halfway up that wall]. How long have you been [activity]?"
5. "Rate the [kind of place in the photo, e.g. that market] out of 10. Trying to decide if it's worth my Saturday."
6. "Your [pet] has main-character energy. What's their name, and who's really in charge?"

## Off her prompts

7. "[Her prompt answer] is a bold claim. I'm going to need [evidence / your top 3]."
8. "Counterpoint to [her take]: [his honest, light opposite take]. Defend yourself."
9. "You said [her prompt answer]. I [true related thing about him]. Pass or fail?"
10. "[Her 'debate me' topic]: I'm on team [side]. Give me your best argument."
11. "[Her 'together we could' plan], agreed. Who's in charge of [one specific part of it]?"
12. "Guess based on your [prompt]: [specific, kind, light guess]. How close am I?"

## Off her bio

13. "[Bio detail] and [second bio detail] in one bio. Which one wins on a free Saturday?"
14. "You mentioned [thing she's into]. What's the one I absolutely have to try first?"
15. "[Her bio line]. Respect. What got you into [it]?"
16. "Your bio raises exactly one question: [specific, light question about a bio detail]?"
17. "Fellow [shared interest that is really in his profile]. [Question only a fellow fan would ask]?"
18. "[Bio detail] is a strong opening line for a bio. Is there a story behind it?"

## Playful fallback (thin profile, not much to work with)

19. "Your profile is a bit of a mystery, so I'll guess: [kind, light guess from a photo]. Right or wrong?"
20. "Quick one: [low-stakes either/or tied to anything visible, e.g. beach or mountains]?"
21. "Settle something for me: [low-stakes debate, e.g. is cereal a soup]?"
22. "I'll go first: [one short true thing about him]. Your turn: [same question for her]?"
23. "Your [one visible detail] is doing a lot of work on this profile. What's the story?"
24. "Not much to go on here, so: [one easy, specific question about the best photo]?"

## After a slow start (she replied briefly, or the match went quiet; one restart only)

25. "Fair, that was a lot of questions. Easier one: [simple either/or tied to her profile]?"
26. "Just walked past a [thing from her profile] and thought of your [prompt]. Verdict still stands?"
27. "Settling a debate with a friend: [low-stakes question related to her]. Need a tiebreaker."
28. "Okay, I've been thinking about your [opinion] for three days. You might be right."
29. "We matched and then both went quiet, so I'll go first: [specific opener from her profile]."
30. "New topic, since [old topic] has run its course: [specific, light question]?"

For the slow-start group: only after a day or two of quiet, only once, and if it gets nothing, let it go.
